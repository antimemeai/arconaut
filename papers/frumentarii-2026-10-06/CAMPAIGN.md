bots manage, guide, accelerate and measure self-driven program/harness evolution.

not authorization to launch all proposed experiments or adopt dependencies.

Three independent lanes acquire/read primary papers and actual reference source:
empirical methods/evaluation; evolving harnesses; orchestration/observability.

claim limitations and concrete Arco consequences. Keep archives intact, extracted
reference trees stripped of Git/filesystem detritus; never execute acquired agents
with operator credentials. Existing corpus reused when useful. Reports and source
manifests stay in each lane's directory; root integrates shared manifest/journal.

is available, continuity/state, useful-work outputs, measurement/attribution,
operator steering, failure/reversal, evidence strength and immediate Arco fit.

from future improvement productivity, portfolio oracle coverage from an executable
policy, and apparent speed from accepted output per wallclock/operator/provider
resources. Review against our actual C++/Lua/audit/context/RRC boundaries and Blackbird.

Deliver actionable matrix, strengths and competing alternatives, concrete next
pilot with useful work/independent outcomes and what research remains uncertain.
No full architecture gate, unbounded benchmark campaign, product mutation, library
adoption, production Python, fleet mutation run or Git publication in this survey.
