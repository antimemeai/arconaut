## Disposition and scoped completion

**Revise**, not promote bounded-first as an unconditional default. Correct useful
outputs shipped, but repeated small-file coding cost increased and recovery results
were mixed. Keep targeted reads as a configurable choice; skip preliminary search
when a known small helper is already the exact question. Keep visible actual feedback,
immutable repair, failure accounting and narrow question/action/outcome records.
Add typed single-mode range helpers from the six concrete failures; their correctness
checked independently, no new pilot speedup claim. Future evaluation would need fresh
tasks, robust returned-error/identity capture, explicit common tool limits and lower
supervisor/recovery overhead. Do NOT collect more now or reuse C/D as unseen.
