### Remaining material fault

**Summary-failure after successful settlement permanently loses the recovery summary and forces a needless reopen** — src/coding.cpp:490-501. The summary `log.original({"session.recovery", …})` is appended *after* the atomic observation batch, inside the same `try`. If the observation batch commits but the summary append fails (e.g. one-record capacity headroom: `append_impl` admits the batch, then the next record trips `capacity`, retained_state.cpp:502-507), the catch calls `block_admission()` and throws even though settlement fully succeeded — admission is closed until reopen for a journal that is actually consistent. Worse, on the next reopen `custody.abandoned` is empty (all attempts are now terminal, so `reconcile` collects none), so the `if (!observations.empty())` guard at coding.cpp:490 skips **both** the batch and the summary: the `session.recovery` summary is never written, yet new turns are accepted. That violates the documented invariant "retain terminal unknown observations **and a recovery summary** before accepting new turns" (docs/SESSION_RECOVERY_SUBPLAN.md:11-12), silently. Fix direction: derive the summary from settled attempts (e.g. detect previously settled-but-unsummarized recoveries on reopen, or write the summary unconditionally keyed off the terminal-unknown observations present), rather than gating it on `abandoned` being non-empty in this process.
